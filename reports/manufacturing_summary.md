# Management summary

Synthetic steel fabrication demonstration; no real plant results or achieved savings.

## Key findings

- Production attainment is 90.8%; aggregate quantities count process-stage completions, not shipped products.
- Machining is the simulated capacity-constraint candidate at 80.6 good units per scheduled hour, with parallel machines pooled.
- WELD_01 has the lowest OEE (66.8%). Check time losses, speed and scrap before deciding why.
- Setup accounts for 40.7% of attributed lost time; the top three categories account for 72.9%.
- The two largest defect categories account for 47.7% of recorded scrap. Shift differences cannot prove a team effect because product mix changes and each team stays on one shift.

## Recommended investigations

- Check the lowest-output operation: review cycle times, product mix and all parallel machines before suggesting more equipment.
- Review the largest downtime categories with maintenance; collect event-level start/end times and distinguish waiting from failures.
- Standardize defect reporting and separate inspection discoveries from the operation that caused the defect.
- Monitor weekly machine-level OEE and attainment with agreed metric definitions; validate proposed interventions using a baseline.

## Limits

Independent machine-shift observations do not model transfers, WIP, part genealogy or flow conservation. Constraint ranking is a capacity-screening proxy, not a proven line bottleneck. See docs/methodology.md.
